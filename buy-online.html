Before you pay

Five things worth checking on the last screen

  • Coverage amount. Super Visa requires at least $100,000 in emergency medical coverage from a Canadian insurer, valid for one year from entry.
  • Effective date. Coverage should start on the day of arrival or departure, not the day you buy — and cannot be backdated once travel has begun.
  • Stability period. Commonly 90, 180 or 365 days. This is where two similarly priced plans differ most.
  • Deductible. Raising it is the most reliable way to lower the premium without losing protection.
  • Names and dates of birth. They must match the passport exactly, or the policy can be challenged at claim time.

Bought the wrong thing? Most policies can be cancelled for a full refund before the effective date, and Super Visa policies are often refundable pro-rata if the visa is refused — see our guide to Super Visa premium refunds.

Buying online — common questions

Can I buy Super Visa insurance online?

Yes. Apply through the visitor-to-Canada links above and you'll normally receive confirmation of insurance by email within minutes. Make sure the policy meets IRCC's requirements: at least $100,000 in emergency medical coverage from a Canadian insurer, valid for one year from the date of entry.

Is it cheaper to buy direct instead of through an advisor?

No. The insurer sets the premium, so the price is identical either way. The difference is whether someone helps you compare the wording — and whether you have someone to call at claim time.

How fast do I get the documents?

Confirmation of insurance and the full policy wording are normally emailed immediately after payment. If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check your junk folder before reapplying — duplicate policies are a nuisance to unwind.

Should I buy online with pre-existing conditions?

You can, but a phone call first is worth the ten minutes. Stability definitions vary by insurer, and an inaccurate answer is the most common reason a large claim is denied. The premium does not change if we help you.

Can I pay monthly?

Some plans offer monthly installments instead of one up-front premium. Availability depends on the insurer, the plan and the length of coverage, and is shown during the application.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. The application and payment take place on the insurer's own secure portal, not on this website. Cover & Protect never sees or stores your card details.

What if I need to change the dates later?

Most insurers allow date changes before coverage begins, and many allow an extension mid-trip if you have not had a claim. Ask before you leave — extending after a medical event is usually not possible.

Not sure which one to buy?

Send the details and we'll tell you which plan actually fits — before you pay. No cost, no obligation, and the premium is the same either way.
