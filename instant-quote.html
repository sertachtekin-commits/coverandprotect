What happens next

You price it, we check it, then you pay

  1. 1 · You build the quoteYou enter the travel dates, ages and province, then compare the plans side by side and pick the coverage limit and deductible that suit you. If the Enhanced plan is selected for someone aged 60 to 85, a medical questionnaire appears inside the quote.
  2. 2 · We review it with youSubmitting the request emails the quote to us. We check the plan actually fits the trip — stability wording, coverage limit, deductible, Super Visa requirements — and raise anything that looks wrong before you commit. This costs nothing and does not change your premium.
  3. 3 · You pay by secure linkWe email you a payment link valid for three hours. It confirms eligibility, carries the medical questionnaire if one is needed, and takes the card details on the insurer's secure page — never over the phone or by email. Your policy documents arrive by email as soon as payment goes through.

Nothing is in force until the premium is paid. A quote is an estimate, subject to the insurer's final terms. If your dates, the traveller's age or the medical answers change, the price can change with them.

Good to know

Before you start

👨‍👩‍👧

Up to six travellers

Individual, companion and family rates on one quote — at most two of whom need a medical questionnaire.

💊

Pre-existing conditions

Only the Enhanced plan can cover stable pre-existing conditions, and it asks medical questions. Standard and Basic do not cover them at all.

🇨🇦

Super Visa applications

Choose at least $100,000 in coverage for one year. A policy can be issued in Pending status so you can file the confirmation with the visa application.

📅

Monthly payments

Qualifying quotes can be paid monthly instead of in one premium — tick the box on the last page. A policy fee applies.

Travelling out of Canada instead? This tool covers visitors to Canada today; outbound coverage for Canadians is being added by the insurer in 2026. In the meantime, use the cost calculator or the options on our buy online page.

Questions about quoting here

Can I buy the policy start to finish in this tool?

Not in one sitting, and that is deliberate. You price the coverage and submit the request here; we review it with you and email a secure payment link. It means someone licensed has looked at the plan before you are on the hook for it — at the same premium you would pay anywhere else.

Does it cost more to quote through an advisor?

No. The insurer sets the premium. What you get in addition is someone to check the wording now and to help with a claim later.

How long is the payment link valid?

Three hours. If it expires, tell us and we'll send a fresh one — nothing you entered is lost.

Who is the insurer?

Quoting and policy issuance are handled by 21st Century Travel Insurance. Cover & Protect is the licensed Ontario agent arranging the coverage, and is who you deal with throughout.

Is my information secure?

The quote runs on the insurer's system inside this page, and payment happens on the insurer's own secure page. Cover & Protect never sees or stores your card details.

What if a medical answer makes someone ineligible?

The tool will say so and let you pick a different plan. Talk to us before changing answers — the right move is usually a different plan, not a different answer.
