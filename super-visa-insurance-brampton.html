Why this matters in Brampton

Your parents will not be covered by OHIP

Brampton is home to one of the largest South Asian communities in Canada, and a great many Super Visa applications each year are for parents and grandparents coming to join family in Peel Region. The insurance requirement catches families out more often than the visa paperwork does.

Visitors to Canada are not covered by OHIP. If your mother or father needs emergency care while staying with you, the bill is private — and emergency treatment, a hospital admission or surgery can reach many thousands of dollars. In Brampton an emergency would most likely mean a trip to Brampton Civic Hospital, with the Peel Memorial Centre for Integrated Health and Wellness handling urgent but non-emergency care. Services at both change from time to time, so confirm current hours and services with William Osler Health System before you need them.

The rules

What the policy has to do

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada sets the insurance condition, and the application is assessed against it. The policy must:

  • Provide at least $100,000 in emergency medical coverage
  • Be valid for at least one year from the date of entry
  • Cover healthcare, hospitalisation and repatriation
  • Be paid in full, with proof of payment available — a quote is not proof of insurance

Requirements are set by IRCC and change from time to time. Confirm the current wording at canada.ca before you buy, and see our guide to policies from insurers outside Canada if a family member has suggested buying abroad.

Cost

What drives the premium

The price is the same in Brampton as anywhere else in Ontario. Insurers rate on the applicant, not on the postal code of the family they are visiting. Four things move the number:

🎂

Age

The single biggest factor. A parent in their fifties and one in their late seventies can pay very different premiums for identical coverage.

💊

Medical history

A plan that covers stable pre-existing conditions costs more than one that excludes them — and is usually worth it.

💰

Deductible

Raising the deductible is the most reliable way to lower the premium without giving up protection.

🛡️

Coverage amount

$100,000 is the minimum. Some families choose more, which raises the premium.

Super Visa insurance in Brampton — common questions

How much is Super Visa insurance in Brampton?

Generally about $1,000 to $6,000 or more a year for the required $100,000 of emergency medical coverage — roughly $90 to $230+ a month. The price is identical in Brampton, Mississauga or anywhere else in Ontario, because insurers rate on the applicant's age, health and coverage chosen rather than the family's address. Age matters more than any other factor.

What does Super Visa insurance cover?

It is emergency medical coverage. It is built to pay for hospital care, emergency doctor's services, diagnostic tests, prescription drugs given during emergency treatment, ambulance transport and repatriation. It is not general health insurance — routine check-ups, dental work, glasses, physiotherapy and elective procedures are normally excluded.

Does Super Visa insurance cover doctor visits?

It covers a doctor's services as part of emergency treatment, such as a physician seeing your parent in the emergency department or during an admission. A routine appointment for a check-up, prescription renewal or ongoing condition is not an emergency and is normally not covered. If your parent needs regular care while here, budget for it separately.

Is Super Visa insurance required?

Yes. IRCC requires proof of private medical insurance as part of the application: at least $100,000 in emergency medical coverage, valid for at least one year from entry, covering healthcare, hospitalisation and repatriation. Confirm current requirements at canada.ca, as they change.

Can I buy Super Visa insurance from India?

The policy traditionally had to come from a Canadian insurer. IRCC has since permitted policies from certain insurers outside Canada, but the conditions are specific and a policy that does not qualify can sink the application. Have the wording checked before relying on it — see our guide to foreign insurer rules.

Can I get a refund if the visa is refused?

Usually yes. Most Canadian insurers refund the premium in full if the application is refused and coverage never started, normally on proof of the refusal letter. After arrival, refunds are typically pro-rata for the unused portion and an administration fee may apply. See Super Visa premium refunds for the detail.

What if my parent has a pre-existing condition?

A managed condition such as controlled blood pressure or diabetes can often still be covered if it has been stable for the period the policy requires — commonly 90, 180 or 365 days. Stability is defined differently by each insurer, and an inaccurate answer on the medical questions is the most common reason a large claim is denied. Worth a phone call before you buy.

How long should I buy the policy for?

At least one year from the date of entry, because that is what IRCC requires. Buying a shorter term to save money is the most common reason an application is returned. If your parents plan to stay longer, most policies can be extended before they expire, provided there has been no claim.
