How to check my licence

You should never take an insurance advisor's word for their own licensing — including mine. In Ontario, every life and health insurance agent is listed in a public registry maintained by FSRA. Search Sertac Tekin or licence number 10112782 at fsrao.ca and you can confirm the licence is active and in good standing before you send anyone a dollar or a medical history.

It takes about a minute, it works for any advisor you are considering, and an advisor who discourages you from doing it has told you something important.

Why going through an advisor doesn't cost you more

This is the question I get most often, usually phrased as "what's your fee?" There isn't one. Insurance premiums in Canada are set by the insurer and filed with the regulator, and the advisor's compensation is built into that premium whether or not an advisor is involved. Buy the identical policy directly from the insurer's website and you pay the identical price — you simply have nobody to call when the wording turns out to matter.

What that means in practice: comparing eight plans with me costs the same as guessing at one on your own. The only thing you spend is the half hour.

What happens on a first call

  • You describe the situation, not the product. Who is travelling, how long for, what conditions they have, and what the visa application needs. You do not need to know what a stability period is — that is my job.
  • I come back with a shortlist, and the reasons. Usually two or three plans, with the differences that actually affect a claim: pre-existing condition wording, the deductible, sub-limits, whether pre-authorisation is required, and how refunds work if the visa is refused.
  • You decide in your own time. Nothing is bound until you say so, and I will tell you plainly when the cheapest plan on the list is the wrong one for your family.
  • If a claim happens, you call me. I help you assemble what the insurer asks for. This is the part that never shows up in a price comparison, and the part people remember.

Can you help if a visiting parent has a pre-existing condition?

Yes, and it is the most common reason families call. The answer depends on the stability period each insurer applies, whether medication or dosage has changed recently, and how the specific policy words the exclusion. Those three things vary a great deal between plans that look identical on price.
